## VLCKit and libVLC

Video preview fallback dynamically loads the unmodified VideoLAN VLCKit 3.7.2 framework, including libVLC and its bundled codecs.

- Official source: https://code.videolan.org/videolan/VLCKit
- VLCKit source revision: 3e42ae47
- Bundled VLC source revision: 79128878
- Binary distribution: https://download.videolan.org/pub/cocoapods/prod/VLCKit-3.7.2-3e42ae47-79128878.tar.xz
- SHA-256: 45fc6398c80d1f8dc0e384a9c80704848e9e82a3a382611bf531fa83c198c276

VLCKit and libVLC are distributed under the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2.1 or later. The upstream distribution's complete COPYING.txt is included as `Contents/Resources/VLCKit-COPYING.txt`. Crawler loads this shared framework dynamically; it does not statically incorporate it. The framework can be replaced with a compatible build and the app re-signed with the developer's signing identity. Upstream source and component notices are available from the source project above.
